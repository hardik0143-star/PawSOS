ABOUT PAWWING SOS

Helping paws. Saving lives. Caring every day.

PawWing SOS brings emergency animal-help contacts and practical everyday pet care into one calm, easy-to-use place.

WHY PAWWING SOS EXISTS

Make the next helpful action easier.

In an emergency, people should be able to quickly find a veterinarian, rescuer or shelter. On ordinary days, PawWing SOS supports people caring for pets, livestock and large animals with feeding basics, grooming, care reminders and trusted learning resources.

The directory is designed to grow responsibly: source-backed starter records are paired with live local discovery and a community option to add missing professional or organisation contacts.
